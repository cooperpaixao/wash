Before you roll in

Disclaimers

These rules keep you, your vehicle and our equipment safe. By using the wash, you agree to them. If anything here is unclear, ask the attendant at the entrance before you go through.

On this page

Safety Vehicles we can't wash What we're not responsible for Express detailing

1Safety

Do not drive through the tunnel.

Shift into neutral and let the conveyor move your vehicle. Drivers will be fully responsible for any damage and injury that may occur when violating this policy.

Do not apply your brakes at any point during the wash.

Braking causes the chain to come off the track, which puts the wash out of service for everyone. Violation of this policy can result in revocation of membership.

2Vehicles we can't wash

Our soft-touch express exterior car wash is designed for everyday road grime. We cannot service excessively soiled vehicles, such as:

  • Vehicles used for "mudding" or off-roading
  • Trucks with debris in the truck bed
  • Vehicles that have had harsh chemicals spilled over or inside them

Attempts to clean excessively soiled vehicles can damage our equipment and ruin the wash experience for the customers behind you.

3What we're not responsible for

Wash 66 is not responsible for damage to:

  • Antennas
  • Antenna moldings
  • Sun visors
  • Bug shields
  • Windshield wipers
  • Mirrors
  • Luggage racks
  • Extended tire valve stems
  • Loose vehicle body parts
  • Existing body or parts damage
  • Non-factory installed items
  • Articles in pickup beds

If your vehicle has parts that are loose, rusted, rotted, cracked or previously damaged, we recommend having them repaired before using our wash.

4Express detailing

Our vehicle detailing packages provide light vehicle cleaning and maintenance. For the safety of our staff, we reserve the right to refuse excessively soiled vehicles. "Excessively soiled" includes, but is not limited to:

  • Excessive garbage
  • Blood
  • Broken glass
  • Mold
  • Vermin infestation
  • Sharp objects

Wash 66 is not responsible for personal items left in the vehicle during detailing.

Questions about any of this? Call 506-847-7627 or email info@wash66.com.
