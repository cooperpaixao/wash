Monthly membership

A tag on your windshield.
A clean car whenever you want.

Pick Good, Better or Best. We stick a small RFID tag on the inside of your windshield, and from then on the wash knows your car. No cards, no codes, no app.

  1. Drive up

    The scanner reads your tag as you pull in. Nothing to tap, nothing to pay.

  2. Follow the attendant

    Someone from our team guides you onto the conveyor track and lines up your wheel.

  3. Shift into neutral

    Hands off the wheel, foot off the brake. The conveyor pulls you through the whole wash.

  4. Drive off clean

    About two minutes later you are out the other end. Pull into a vacuum bay if you like. It's free.

Built for Maritime winters

Road salt doesn't stop at the paint.

From November to April, every trip down the Hampton Road or out to Route 1 puts a fresh coat of salt and brine on your car. Most of it ends up underneath, where you can't see it and the rain won't rinse it off.

Left there, it eats at brake lines, rocker panels, wheel wells and exhaust. Our Better and Best washes include an underbody flush with rust inhibitor, and with a membership, washing after every storm doesn't cost a thing extra.

  • 01

    Wash after every storm, not just once in spring. Salt does its damage while it sits.

  • 02

    Pick a wash with an underbody flush from the first snow until the street sweepers come out.

  • 03

    On a really cold day, open and close your doors once after the wash so the seals don't freeze shut.

Questions

Good to know before you roll in.

Anything else, call us at 506-847-7627 or ask whoever is at the entrance.

How often can I wash with a membership?

Once a day, every day, at the tier you signed up for.

How long does a wash take?

Usually about two minutes through the tunnel. Right after a snowstorm there can be a short line, since everyone has the same idea.

Do I stay in my car?

Yes. Once the attendant has you lined up, shift into neutral, take your hands off the wheel and keep your foot off the brake. Braking can knock the conveyor chain off its track, which shuts the wash down for everyone behind you.

Are the vacuums really free?

Yes. They're free with any wash you buy, and free every visit with a membership. The bays are covered, so they work year round.

Is it a touchless wash?

No, it's soft-touch. After the pre-soak, gentle brushes do the scrubbing, which is what lifts the road film that water pressure alone tends to leave behind.

Can any vehicle go through?

Most everyday cars, SUVs and pickups are fine. We can't take vehicles caked in mud from off-roading, trucks with loose debris in the bed, or anything with loose or damaged parts. The full list is on our disclaimers page, or call if you're not sure.

How do I sign up for a membership?

Come by the wash and we'll set you up on the spot. We put your RFID tag on the windshield before your first wash, so you can use it right away.
